HEEJI YOON

I am 1st year MS. student in CVLAB@KAIST AI under the advisement of Prof.Seungryong Kim.

My research interest lies in understanding real world at multiple levels through the visual information, with the goal of solving practical problems. In particular, my current research focuses on segmentation and multimodal large language models (MLLMs), while I also hold a strong interest in medical AI.

Before joining KAIST, I received my B.S in Life Science from Korea University, where I was particularly fascinated by neuroscience. I remain deeply intrigued by the analogies between the human neurobiological system and artificial intelligence, and I look forward to the synergy they will continue to create.

If you have any questions or would like to connect, feel free to reach out via email (hjyoon@kaist.ac.kr) or LinkedIn!
